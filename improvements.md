# Improvements & Roadmap

### Cloud Sync
- Encrypted database for cross-device synchronization.
- Secure, private login system.

### Integrations
- Music Previews: Play audio snippets directly within the shelf.
- Watchlist Sync: Auto-fetch data from services like Netflix or Hulu.
- AI Tagging: Smart content summaries and automated categorization.

### Utility
- Browser Extension: Save links with a single click without opening the app.
- Bulk Actions: Select multiple items to delete, tag, or move.
